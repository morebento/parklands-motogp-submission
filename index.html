Write your own submission on the MotoGP circuit proposal

The SA Government is inviting feedback on a plan to permanently redevelop Victoria Park/Pakapakanthi for MotoGP. This tool helps you turn your own concerns into a clear, personal submission in a few minutes — then hands you off to the official channels to send it yourself.

Consultation closes 11:59pm, Sunday 20 September 2026 (Adelaide time).

    Nothing you type here is sent to a server, logged, or stored anywhere outside this browser tab. See “Your privacy” in the footer for details.

      Your privacy
      
      
        Nothing you type into this tool is ever sent to a server, logged, or stored outside your
        own browser tab. There is no backend and no database — this is a static page that runs
        entirely on your device. No cookies are used. Refreshing or closing this tab clears
        everything you've entered.
